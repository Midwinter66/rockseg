Figure 1. Overall research workflow. The main pipeline (left) processes DOM2 through physical-scale multi-scale segmentation, within-scale and cross-scale deduplication, 2D-3D association, ground-referenced 2.5D surface reconstruction, and frozen Shape-Aware V2 LightGBM correction to produce representative volume estimates for 4,000 accepted DOM2 rocks. The external model development branch (right) trains the correction model on 465 scaled OBJ meshes. The external test MAPE of 5.82% applies to scaled external mesh validation only; the 90.98% completion rate is a pipeline outcome, not volume accuracy. No per-rock real-mine ground-truth volume is available.
