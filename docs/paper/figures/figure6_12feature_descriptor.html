Figure 6. Canonical 12-feature Shape-Aware descriptor, grouped into footprint geometry (features 1-5), normalized height statistics (features 6-10), and volume ratios (features 11-12). The feature order is fixed for both training and mine inference. Feature 10 (H_skew_norm) is defined as the raw height skewness H_skew, not H_skew divided by H. The model contract uses y_ratio = V_true / V_2.5D as the external training target and V_pred = V_2.5D * y_pred as the corrected mine estimate. Feature consistency between training and inference was verified with zero difference on checked samples.
